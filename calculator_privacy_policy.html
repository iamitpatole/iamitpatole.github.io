01. Application Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Calculator (Package Name: com.bytekeeperlabs.calculator), developed and maintained by ByteKeeper Labs ("we", "our", or "us").

The application is an all-in-one utility providing standard arithmetic operations, Indian number formatting (Lakhs & Crores), and financial & utility calculators (including GST, EMI, Compound/Simple Interest, SIP, Discount, Percentage, Number to Words, Age, Date Difference, Split Bill, and Profit & Loss).

02. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII).

When you use the Calculator app:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • We do not access or collect precise or coarse location data.
  • We do not gather hardware identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, Android Advertising ID).
  • We do not record keystrokes, contact lists, photos, or media files.
  • We do not collect runtime usage statistics, telemetry, crash dumps, or behavioral analytics.
Guaranteed Data Sovereignty: You do not need to register, create an account, or link any social profile to use any feature of this application.

03. On-Device Local Data Storage

Any data created during the usage of the Calculator is saved strictly on your local device via Android's protected application sandbox (using standard SharedPreferences):

  • Calculation History: Past mathematical expressions and results generated by you so you can review or reuse previous calculations.
  • User Preferences: Your customized preferences such as Theme Mode (Light/Dark/System), Indian Number Formatting toggle, Decimal Precision, and Sound/Haptic feedback toggles.

This data is never transmitted over the internet, never backed up to remote proprietary servers, and cannot be accessed by other applications on your device.

04. Device Permissions

The Calculator application is designed to be lightweight and strictly respectful of device security:

  • No Dangerous Permissions: The app does not request access to Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Location, SMS, or Phone State.
  • Vibrate (Haptics): The application requests the basic vibration permission purely to provide tactile haptic feedback when tapping buttons, which can be toggled off at any time in Settings.
  • No Internet Permission: The core calculator functions autonomously without requiring network calls or cloud connections.

05. Third-Party Services & Tracking

Unlike many utility apps, Calculator contains zero third-party tracking or advertising frameworks:

  • No third-party advertising SDKs (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin).
  • No tracking or behavioral analytics libraries (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Adjust, AppsFlyer).
  • No third-party social media plugins or data-sharing APIs.

Because no third-party SDKs are embedded, your usage patterns and device attributes cannot be monetized, profiled, or traded by third parties.

06. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13 specifically, but it is entirely safe for users of all ages, including children and students. Because we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, our application fully complies with the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Google Play Families Policy.

07. User Data Control & Deletion

You have total and absolute control over all data stored by the app on your phone:

  • Clear History: You can delete individual calculation records by tapping the delete icon, or clear the entire history log at once by tapping the trash icon in the History tab.
  • Reset Preferences: You can reset all preferences back to factory defaults at any time via the Settings screen.
  • Permanent Deletion: Clearing the app's cache/storage in your device's Android App Settings or uninstalling the app permanently purges all stored preferences and history from the device.

08. Security Standards

We value your trust. Since no data leaves your mobile device, the risk of interception or server-side data breaches is eliminated at architectural level. Local storage is protected by Android's internal application sandboxing, which prevents other installed applications from reading your data.
